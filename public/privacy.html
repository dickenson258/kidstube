隐私政策

朵米片单（DomiBuddy Lists）网站与朵米助手（DomiBuddy Helper）浏览器扩展 · 更新于 2026-11-12 · English

一句话

我们不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息。孩子的片单和观看记录只保存在你自己的浏览器里。

朵米片单网站

  • 视频片单、分组、家长密码（只存哈希值）、每日观看时长和观看进度，都只保存在本浏览器的本地存储（localStorage）里，不发送到我们的服务器。
  • 不使用任何统计、广告或第三方追踪代码。
  • 播放视频时，视频由 YouTube、哔哩哔哩等网站的官方播放器加载，这些网站按照它们自己的隐私政策处理播放数据。
  • "分享片单"生成的链接里只有视频编号、标题、封面和合集信息，不包含孩子的观看记录或任何家庭信息。链接内容由你自己决定发给谁。

朵米助手浏览器扩展

  • 只在朵米片单的网址上工作（lists.domibuddy.com 等），其他网页上什么都不做。每个网址第一次使用时需要你确认允许。
  • 它替朵米片单网页向视频网站（哔哩哔哩、YouTube、腾讯视频、优酷、百度网盘等）请求视频标题、封面、合集列表和播放地址。请求会带上你在这些网站上的登录状态（Cookie 由浏览器自动附带），扩展和网页都读取不到 Cookie 的内容。
  • 请求结果只交给发出请求的朵米片单页面，不保存，也不发送给我们或任何第三方。
  • 扩展只在本地保存一项设置：你已经允许过的朵米片单网址列表。
  • 扩展不收集浏览记录、个人身份信息、位置、通讯内容、健康或财务信息，也不用于广告或信用评估。

儿童隐私

朵米片单由家长设置和管理。我们不向儿童收集姓名、年龄、照片、联系方式或任何其他个人信息，也不对儿童做任何画像或推荐。

联系我们

对隐私有任何疑问，请在 GitHub 上提交问题。

Privacy Policy (English)

DomiBuddy Lists (朵米片单) website and DomiBuddy Helper (朵米助手) browser extension · Updated 2026-11-12

In short: we don't collect, upload or sell any personal information. Your child's lists and watch history stay in your own browser.

The website

  • Video lists, groups, the parent PIN (stored only as a hash), daily watch time and progress are kept in this browser's local storage and are never sent to our servers.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code.
  • Videos play in the official players of YouTube, Bilibili and other sites, which handle playback data under their own privacy policies.
  • A shared list link holds only video IDs, titles, thumbnails and series info, never watch history or family information. You decide who receives it.

The browser extension

  • It runs only on DomiBuddy sites (such as lists.domibuddy.com) and does nothing on other pages. Each site must be allowed by you the first time.
  • It requests video titles, thumbnails, collection lists and stream addresses from video sites (Bilibili, YouTube, Tencent Video, Youku, Baidu Netdisk and others) on behalf of the DomiBuddy page, using your own sign-ins on those sites. The browser attaches cookies automatically; neither the extension nor the page can read them.
  • Results go only to the DomiBuddy page that asked for them. Nothing is stored or sent to us or any third party.
  • The only thing the extension stores locally is the list of DomiBuddy sites you have allowed.
  • It does not collect browsing history, personally identifiable information, location, communications, health or financial data, and is not used for advertising or creditworthiness.

Children's privacy

DomiBuddy Lists is set up and managed by parents. We do not collect names, ages, photos, contact details or any other personal information from children, and we do not profile children or make recommendations to them.

Contact

For any privacy question, please open an issue on GitHub.
